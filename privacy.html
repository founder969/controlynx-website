Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026  ·  Controlynx.ai

1. Introduction

Controlynx.ai ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website and services.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact information: name, work email address, company name, and job role — collected when you submit a demo request or early access form.
  • Usage data: pages visited, time on site, and interaction data collected via Google Analytics (anonymised).
  • Project data: if you use the Controlynx platform, we process project controls data you upload or submit. This data belongs to you and is never shared.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to demo requests and early access enquiries.
  • To communicate about product updates relevant to your request.
  • To improve the website and platform based on anonymised usage data.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Form submissions are processed via Formspree and delivered to our company email. We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

Your project data is never used to train AI models, shared with other customers, or retained beyond the period necessary to provide our service.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR, the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL), or other applicable data protection laws, including:

  • The right to access data we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction or deletion of your data.
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at founder@controlynx.ai.

6. Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This sets cookies to collect anonymised traffic data. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Formspree — processes form submissions. See Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics — anonymised usage tracking. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — website hosting. See Vercel Privacy Policy.
